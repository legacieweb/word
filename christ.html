Jesus as God: Biblical Verses and Hints

This page lists key Bible verses that directly or indirectly affirm Jesus as God, along with hints from Scripture that point to His divinity.

Verses That Affirm Jesus is God

  • John 1:1 - "In the beginning was the Word, and the Word was with God, and the Word was God."
  • John 1:14 - "The Word became flesh and made his dwelling among us. We have seen his glory, the glory of the one and only Son, who came from the Father, full of grace and truth."
  • John 8:58 - "Very truly I tell you," Jesus answered, "before Abraham was born, I am!"
  • John 10:30 - "I and the Father are one."
  • John 14:9 - "Jesus answered: 'Don't you know me, Philip, even after I have been among you such a long time? Anyone who has seen me has seen the Father. How can you say, "Show us the Father"?'"
  • Colossians 2:9 - "For in Christ all the fullness of the Deity lives in bodily form."
  • Hebrews 1:8 - "But about the Son he says, 'Your throne, O God, will last for ever and ever; a scepter of justice will be the scepter of your kingdom.'"
  • Revelation 1:8 - "'I am the Alpha and the Omega,' says the Lord God, 'who is, and who was, and who is to come, the Almighty.'"
  • Revelation 22:13 - "I am the Alpha and the Omega, the First and the Last, the Beginning and the End."
  • Isaiah 9:6 (prophecy fulfilled in Jesus) - "For to us a child is born, to us a son is given, and the government will be on his shoulders. And he will be called Wonderful Counselor, Mighty God, Everlasting Father, Prince of Peace."
  • Micah 5:2 (prophecy) - "But you, Bethlehem Ephrathah, though you are little among the thousands of Judah, yet out of you shall come forth to Me the One to be Ruler in Israel, whose goings forth are from of old, from everlasting."
  • 1 Timothy 3:16 - "Beyond all question, the mystery from which true godliness springs is great: He appeared in the flesh, was vindicated by the Spirit, was seen by angels, was preached among the nations, was believed on in the world, was taken up in glory."

Hints in the Bible That Point to Jesus as God

  • Jesus' Miracles and Authority - Jesus performed miracles like turning water into wine (John 2:1-11), healing the sick (Matthew 4:23-24), raising the dead (John 11:43-44), and walking on water (Matthew 14:25-33), demonstrating divine power.
  • Jesus' Claims - Jesus claimed to be the Son of God (Matthew 16:16-17) and that salvation comes through Him alone (John 14:6).
  • Worship of Jesus - People worshipped Jesus (Matthew 14:33, John 20:28), and He accepted it, which would be blasphemy if He were not God.
  • Jesus' Resurrection - Jesus rose from the dead (Matthew 28:5-6), fulfilling prophecies and proving His divine nature.
  • Old Testament Prophecies - Many OT prophecies point to a divine Messiah, such as Psalm 2:7 ("You are my son; today I have become your father") and Isaiah 7:14 ("Therefore the Lord himself will give you a sign: The virgin will conceive and give birth to a son, and will call him Immanuel").
  • Jesus' Role in Creation - Colossians 1:16 states, "For in him all things were created: things in heaven and on earth, visible and invisible, whether thrones or powers or rulers or authorities; all things have been created through him and for him."
  • Jesus' Forgiveness of Sins - Jesus forgave sins (Mark 2:5-12), a power only God has.
  • Jesus' Second Coming - Jesus will return in glory (Matthew 24:30-31), as described in divine terms.
  • Jesus' Titles - Titles like "Son of Man" (Daniel 7:13-14), "King of Kings" (Revelation 19:16), and "Lord" (Philippians 2:9-11) point to His divinity.
  • The Holy Spirit's Testimony - The Spirit testifies about Jesus (John 15:26), and Jesus sends the Spirit (John 14:26).
